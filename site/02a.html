Exact processing method and numerical limits

1. Select and demodulate

Select the maximum complex temporal variance inside a fixed 0.8–2.2 m range gate. Extract atan2(Q, I), then unwrap nearest-neighbor phase differences. Manual selection is exploratory; the belt does not choose the bin.

2. Detrend and filter

Remove a least-squares linear trend. Apply a fourth-order Butterworth bandpass design, 0.08–0.70 Hz at 50 Hz, in second-order sections, forward then backward. This is noncausal offline processing, not a real-time device.

3. Compare without hiding error

Trim 2 s per edge; use the retained 26 s, a symmetric Hann window and native Fourier bins. Display signed zero-lag Pearson correlation without a polarity flip or lag optimization. The spectral grid is approximately 2.31 breaths/min.

The trim is an illustrative edge policy, not proof that low-frequency transients have vanished. Phase and belt are normalized independently; no calibrated displacement, lung-volume conversion, diagnosis or population accuracy is inferred from one segment.
