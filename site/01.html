What is in the archive?

Complex range-FFT arrays, radar timestamps and chirp configuration; ECG, accelerometer and instructed breath-hold intervals.

These are not raw ADC samples. Most recordings span 50 s; some span 60 s. Radar and reference sensors share a PC timebase, with residual delay.

What makes it realistic?

Posture, post-exercise physiology, a varied cohort, breath holds and documented data-quality defects.

Twelve recordings have initial zero-valued radar frames. Device/antenna geometry differs from our original ideal transverse ULA.

A realistic study includes the acquisition defects—not only the cleanest trace.
