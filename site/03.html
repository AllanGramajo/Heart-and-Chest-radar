What chest motion can and cannot establish

Chest motion is a measurement.
Lung function is an inference.

A radar return does not become a ventilation measurement just because we display it over a chest-shaped grid.

Why carrier frequency, bandwidth and frame rate are different

Carrier, bandwidth and frame rate solve different problems.

Do not copy the original simulator’s constants into a dataset with a different acquisition configuration.

Phase sensitivity
φ = 4πd / λ
d = λφ / 4π

Single coherent monostatic reflector model. Phase depends on round-trip path; clutter and multiple scatterers complicate displacement recovery.

A fine phase measurement is not fine range resolution.

Range resolution
ΔR ≈ c / (2B)

Use effective measured bandwidth, not the radar chip’s maximum specification.

Range-bin spacing is not independent-target resolution.

MobiVital: 5.14 cm bin spacing; reported 1.4 GHz bandwidth gives about 10.7 cm nominal resolution.

Phase tracking
|Δφ| < π
|Δd| < λ / 4

Ideal nearest-neighbor unwrapping condition, not a guarantee under noise or multipath.

At roughly 60 GHz and 10 Hz frames: about 12.5 mm/s of radial speed.

A sampling increase does not eliminate nulls, body motion, range migration or phase bias.

Slow breathing does not mean every disturbance is safely sampled.

Where an ECG model belongs

ECG fusion is a research question—not a feature we already built.

Paired electrical and mechanical signals make a useful hypothesis. They do not establish clinical benefit by themselves.

ECG-only

Prespecified clinical or physiological target. Train and evaluate on the same participant split used by every model.

Radar-only

Motion-derived features plus signal-quality indicators. Preserve missingness and low-confidence outputs.

ECG + radar

Test whether the additional modality improves held-out performance, calibration or robustness.

Needed for a meaningful comparison

Same participants, aligned time intervals, the same outcome labels, leakage-free model selection and an external test set.

Not an acceptable shortcut

Pairing an unrelated ECG database with unrelated radar subjects and presenting it as synchronized multimodal training data.

First validate motion and timing. Then test whether they add information.
